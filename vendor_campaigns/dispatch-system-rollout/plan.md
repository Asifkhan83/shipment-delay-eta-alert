# Campaign Plan: Vendor Rollout of the Automated Dispatch and Inventory Reporting System

**Company:** Apex Transit Solutions (sample company, for practice)
**Made with:** `/marketing:campaign-plan`
**Voice:** follows `operations_notes/voice_guidelines.md` (Factual. Calm. Clear.)
**Campaign window:** Monday, December 7, 2026 to Sunday, January 3, 2027 (4 weeks)
**Channel:** email only. No physical mailers, no live training webinars.

> **Practice only.** Every vendor address in this course uses `@example.com`. No email in this plan is sent to a real person.

---

## 1. Campaign overview

**Campaign name:** *Switch to Apex Dispatch*

**In one sentence:** a 4-week email sequence that tells each active vendor what is changing, what they need to do, and how to get help, so they can move to the new automated dispatch and inventory reporting system before the end of the year.

**Primary goal (SMART):** 120 of our 150 active vendors (80%) have **transitioned** by Sunday, January 3, 2027.

**What "transitioned" means (agreed):** the vendor has (1) activated their account in the new system **and** (2) submitted at least one dispatch request, advance shipping notice (ASN) or inventory report through it. Activating an account without using it does not count.

**Secondary goals**
- Every one of the 150 vendors receives the first email and knows the change is happening.
- Each of the three vendor groups reaches at least 70%, so no group is left behind.
- Vendor questions are answered by a person within one business day.

## 2. Target audience

150 active vendors and partners in three groups (sample split for practice: 30 / 70 / 50).

| Group | Vendors | Who they are | What they send us today | What they care about | Main worry |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A. High-volume freight carriers** | 30 | Larger trucking companies moving full truckload (FTL) and large less-than-truckload (LTL) freight | Pickup confirmations, bill of lading (BOL), proof of delivery (POD) | Speed, few manual steps, fitting it into their own dispatch systems | "Will this add work for our dispatchers or clash with our own system?" |
| **B. Local LTL couriers** | 70 | Smaller regional couriers, often a few trucks and a small office | Pickup times, delivery status, POD | Simple steps, works on a phone, no new cost | "Is this complicated? Do we need new software?" |
| **C. Packaging supply vendors** | 50 | Suppliers sending boxes, pallets, wrap and labels to our warehouse | ASNs, dock appointment requests, delivery confirmations | Getting dock appointments easily, clear inventory needs | "Will we still get our dock slots and orders on time?" |

**Short profile:** a dispatcher or account contact at a carrier or supplier who handles Apex shipments alongside many other customers. They need to know exactly what changes, by when, and what to do, in as few steps as possible. They read email on desktop and phone, often between loads.

**Stage:** they already work with Apex. This is a **change and adoption** campaign, not a sales one: they move from "aware" to "set up" to "using it".

## 3. Key messages

**Core message:** *From December 7, Apex dispatch requests, ASNs and inventory reports move to one online system. Set up your account in about 15 minutes, and send your next update there.*

**Supporting messages**

| # | Message | Proof point (verified source needed before use) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **What changes:** dispatch requests, ASNs, dock appointments and inventory reports go through one system instead of email and phone. | System feature list from the Apex operations team. |
| 2 | **What you need to do:** activate your account, then send your next ASN or dispatch update through the system. | Step-by-step setup guide (to be written, see section 6). |
| 3 | **What it means for you:** you see your dock appointments and request status in one place. | Only claims confirmed by operations. No time-saving numbers unless measured. |
| 4 | **Help is available:** reply to any email, or call the dispatch team (Mon–Fri, 7 am–7 pm). | Support contact from the voice guide. |

**Message by group**
- **Carriers (A):** focus on pickups, BOL and POD upload, and how it fits their dispatch routine.
- **LTL couriers (B):** focus on how few steps it takes and that it works in a phone's web browser with nothing to install.
- **Packaging vendors (C):** focus on ASNs and booking dock appointments.

**Voice rules for every email** (from `operations_notes/voice_guidelines.md`)
- Exact dates, never "soon" or "ASAP".
- No hype words: no "seamless", "world-class" or "lightning-fast".
- No guarantees: never "guaranteed dock slots" or "this will save you 5 hours". State only what operations has confirmed.
- No promises on behalf of a carrier or vendor.
- Vendor terms are fine (ASN, BOL, POD, LTL, FTL, dock appointment, cut-off time). Spell each out the first time it appears.
- Internal terms stay out (cross-dock, pick and pack, cycle count).
- Every email ends with the automated sign-off:

```
— Apex Transit Solutions Dispatch Team
This update was sent automatically from our tracking system.
Questions? Reply to this email or call (555) 010-0000 (Mon–Fri, 7 am–7 pm).
```

No budget was given. The plan uses only email and existing staff. The main cost is staff time:

## 9. Risks and mitigations

| Risk | What could happen | What we do |
|---|---|---|
| **Holidays** | Vendors are short-staffed or closed Dec 24–Jan 1, and late transitions stall. | Front-load the work: target 100 transitioned by Dec 23. No emails on holidays. Holiday hours in E5. |
| **System not ready by Dec 7** | Vendors click the link and it doesn't work, and trust drops. | Go/no-go check on Thu, Dec 3. If it's not ready, delay E1. Don't send on a fixed date regardless. |
| **Wrong or outdated contacts** | Emails bounce or reach the wrong person. | Clean the vendor list before launch. Check bounces daily in week 1. |
| **Large carriers want a system link-up, not manual entry** | Group A delays because they want their own system connected. | Flag early. Their reply goes to operations, and email never promises a link-up date. |
| **Small couriers find it hard** | Group B falls behind. | Short, phone-friendly guide. Reply-for-help offered in every email. Watch group B's numbers weekly. |
| **Promises we can't keep** | An email promises dock slots or time savings. | Every email is checked against the voice guide's banned claims before use. |
